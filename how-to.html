How do I open StockStart?

StockStart runs in your web browser. You can also install it on Windows.

  1. In a browser: go to app.getstockstart.com and sign in. New here? Use Start free trial.
  2. On Windows: open StockStart in the Microsoft Store and install it. The Windows app opens the same StockStart. Sign in with the same email and password.

Want to look around first? On a larger screen, click Run guided demo in the workspace. It loads a small sample pricebook and sample invoice lines.

How do I upload my ServiceTitan pricebook export?

StockStart compares every invoice line with your current pricebook, so load the pricebook first.

  1. Export your pricebook from ServiceTitan as a CSV or XLSX file.
  2. In StockStart, find Step 1 · ServiceTitan pricebook and click Upload pricebook.
  3. Choose the file. StockStart shows how many pricebook items it loaded.

If it says no usable rows were found, check that the file has a Code (or SKU) column and a Description column. Get template downloads a sample pricebook CSV with the columns it reads.

How do I upload supplier invoices?

StockStart reads these file types: PDF (text PDFs, and scanned pages with OCR), photos (PNG, JPG, JPEG), and spreadsheets (CSV, XLS, XLSX).

  1. Click Add evidence (at the top, or under Step 2), or Add files in the Evidence inbox.
  2. Pick one file or several at once.
  3. Each file shows in the Evidence inbox with the supplier it found and how many lines it read. A file it could not read is marked as an error, with the reason.

Have an emailed invoice you can copy text from? Open any document in the inbox, use Paste lines from an electronic invoice, and click Add pasted lines. Put part number, description, quantity and unit cost on each line, separated by tabs or |.

Files are read in your browser. Lines read from photos and scanned pages must be checked against the original before they can be approved. StockStart does not claim perfect OCR.

How do I match supplier invoices to my pricebook?

Matching happens on its own as soon as a pricebook and invoices are loaded. Your job is to check the results.

  1. Go to the Item library. Each row is one item, with its pricebook code, the invoice descriptions found, the quantity and the cost from the invoices.
  2. A green dot means Ready. An amber dot means it needs your review.
  3. Click the Review tab (it shows how many are waiting), or the Needs review box, to see only those items.
  4. Click an item. The Human review panel shows why it is waiting, the descriptions found and which file and supplier each line came from.
  5. If it is right, click Confirm group. StockStart moves to the next item.

Every decision is listed under Decision history. When nothing is left, the panel says Review complete.

Before you trust a match, open the line in the Evidence inbox and set its Evidence role, for example Confirmed purchase, Credit / return or Duplicate. Lines left as Unknown — review cannot be exported.

How do I spot a supplier price increase for my ServiceTitan pricebook?

  1. In the Item library, the cost read from the invoices is shown under each item's quantity. If invoices show different costs for the same item, you see a range, for example $12.25–$12.49.
  2. Use Search normalized items to find a part by name or code.
  3. When you update your Materials workbook (see section 10), the message on screen lists each changed item with its old pricebook cost and the new invoice cost.
StockStart shows the old and new cost in dollars; it does not work out the percent change. To see the change in percent, paste the lines into the free pricebook unit-cost checker. It shows the change per unit and flags a box price entered as the price of one. For common questions, see supplier price changes FAQ.

How do I fix an item StockStart couldn't match?

Items it could not match get a code starting with NEW- and wait in review. The amber note at the top of the Human review panel says why, for example No confident pricebook match or Confirm suggested pricebook item.

  1. It is a part you already have: open Choose a different pricebook item, pick the right one, and it is matched to that code.
  2. Two different parts were grouped together: click Keep separate. Each line becomes its own item.
  3. It really is a new part: check the details, then click Confirm group.
  4. A value was read wrong (part number, quantity, unit, pack quantity or unit cost): in the Evidence inbox, click Review lines → on that file. Correct the line while looking at the original. Enter your name in Reviewer name and a Reason or supporting reference. Tick I checked these changes against the source. Click Save review and recalculate.

For lines read from a photo or scan, also tick I verified every extracted field against the original on each line. If an item has a safety problem, such as a unit-of-measure conflict, Confirm group will not approve it until the evidence is corrected.

How do I keep my work for later?

  1. Click Save workspace on device. This saves the pricebook, original files and unsaved drafts in this browser.
  2. Next time, on the same computer and browser, click Open last saved workspace.
Save again after each round of changes. This is browser storage on that one computer, not a cloud backup. StockStart warns you before you leave the page with work open.

To share progress, Print / PDF prints the review, and Email summary opens your email program with a short summary: items ready, items still to review, and items blocked.

How do I export a ServiceTitan pricebook update?

Only items marked Ready are exported. Items still in review are never included. There are two downloads: a CSV of approved items with their latest costs (below), and your own Materials workbook with new parts added and existing costs updated (next section).

  1. Click Export library (or Export result).
  2. You get stockstart-normalized-item-library.csv. It lists every ready item with its Code, description, category, unit, quantity, LatestCost, supplier part numbers and the source files.

LatestCost is left blank when the invoices disagree on the cost. To update costs in ServiceTitan, use the workbook step below.

How do I add new parts and update costs in my ServiceTitan pricebook?

StockStart adds approved new parts to your own exported ServiceTitan Materials workbook and updates the Cost of parts already in it. You then import that workbook.

  1. Export your Materials workbook (.xlsx) from ServiceTitan. It must have a sheet named Materials.
  2. In StockStart, go to Patch approved items into your tenant-exported workbook.
  3. Type your Account and Cost of Sales Account names exactly as they appear in your ServiceTitan export.
  4. Choose Active and Taxable (Yes or No). Inventory can be left blank. Enter $ Bonus and % Bonus (0 is fine).
  5. Click Choose exported .xlsx (or Prepare import) and pick that workbook.
  6. You get stockstart-servicetitan-import.xlsx. New ready items are added as new rows at the bottom of the Materials sheet. Ready items already in the sheet get their Cost (and a matching vendor price column, if there is one) changed to the invoice cost. The message on screen lists each old and new cost. The rest of your workbook is left as it was.
For an item already in the workbook, only its Cost and matching vendor price change. StockStart leaves the cost alone and shows a warning when the invoices disagree on the cost, the invoice unit differs from the row's unit, the code is listed twice, or the cost cell is a formula. If nothing would change, nothing is downloaded. Each new item also needs a category. Workbooks that use an Excel table on the Materials sheet, or that are digitally signed, are refused.

How does a ServiceTitan pricebook import from invoices work?

You import it yourself. StockStart does not connect to, read from or write to your ServiceTitan account.

  1. Open the downloaded file and check a few rows: code, unit, cost and category.
  2. Import the workbook into ServiceTitan the same way you import your own pricebook files.
  3. Open three or four items in ServiceTitan afterward and check them.
  4. Next time, export a fresh pricebook from ServiceTitan and upload that, so StockStart starts from what you approved.

Before you import, run through the ServiceTitan pricebook import checklist. For the full weekly routine, see the pricebook guide, or the manual method if you prefer to do it by hand.

Common questions

How do I keep pricebook prices up to date?

Each time new supplier invoices arrive, export a fresh pricebook from ServiceTitan, upload it to StockStart with the new invoices, review what it flags, and export the update. Weekly or monthly, whatever your buying rhythm is.

Is StockStart for HVAC pricebook management and plumbing pricebook updates?

Yes, for shops on ServiceTitan, including HVAC, plumbing and electrical. It keeps the pricebook current from supplier invoices. It is not an inventory system: invoice quantities are purchases, not stock on hand.

Does StockStart change my ServiceTitan pricebook by itself?

No. StockStart is independent software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by ServiceTitan. It prepares reviewed files; you import them yourself.
