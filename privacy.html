Summary

THE SHORT VERSIONStockStart reads your documents in your browser. Your invoices, pricebook exports and item library are not sent to our servers and are not stored there. Our servers keep only your account, sign-in and billing details. We don't sell data or train models on it.

This policy explains what Profitpin LLC collects when you visit getstockstart.com, create an account, use the StockStart application, or contact support, and what we do with it.

Account information

To create an account we collect your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash). We also record when you accepted the Terms, when you verified your email, and your trial start and end dates. Stripe collects your name, billing address and card at checkout; from Stripe we keep your customer and subscription identifiers, subscription status and billing dates. We never see your full card number.

Documents you open in StockStart

Invoices, receipts, photographs, spreadsheets and pricebook exports you open in StockStart are read in your browser, on your device. Text reading (OCR) and matching also run in your browser. The files, the text read from them, your item library, your review decisions and your exports are not sent to our servers, and we do not store them. They may contain supplier names, prices, quantities, and sometimes personal information such as a sales rep's name or an address on an invoice.

If you choose Save workspace on device, a copy of your workspace, including your original files, is kept in that browser's storage on that device. It is kept separately for each account email. We cannot see it, it does not sync to other devices, and clearing the browser's data for StockStart deletes it. Files you download are saved where your browser saves them.

To read images, your browser downloads the open-source OCR engine and its language files from jsDelivr, a public file server. Your documents are not sent there.

We do not sell Customer Data, use it for advertising, or use it to train machine-learning models.

Usage and technical data

Our servers keep ordinary logs: request time, path, IP address, browser type, and account identifier, retained for a short period for security and troubleshooting. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in. When you sign up or sign in, our server records that event with your email address in our own internal dashboard. We don't run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the application or the website. We count page visits ourselves, without cookies and without storing your IP address: each count records only the page, the day and how you arrived (for example a search engine, Product Hunt or one of our emails), and your browser keeps that arrival source for up to 90 days so a later sign-up can be credited to it. If you follow a link in an email we sent to your business, the link carries a short code naming your business, so we can see that your business visited, whether someone stayed to read the page (about ten seconds plus a scroll, tap or mouse movement), and whether you used the free tool. You can remove that code from the link before opening it. We never track whether an email was opened. When StockStart is installed as an app (for example from the Microsoft Store), the first time it opens it sends one anonymous count to the same counter saying the installed app was opened; it carries no account details.

How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Your documents stay on your device, so other customers cannot see them. Anyone who can use that device and browser profile could open a saved workspace, so protect the device. Card data is entered on Stripe's pages under Stripe's PCI DSS certification. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the Service. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you as the law requires.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and billing details while you have an account. Your documents and item library are not on our servers, so we have none of them to keep or delete. A saved workspace stays in your browser until you clear the browser's data for StockStart. When a trial or subscription ends, paid features require an active subscription; expiry does not delete a saved workspace. To delete your account, email support from your account address. We delete it within 30 days, except what tax and accounting rules require us to keep (billing records, generally seven years) and short-lived backups that roll off on their own schedule.

Emails we send

Transactional only: your email verification code, receipts and billing emails from Stripe if you subscribe, notices about changes to price or terms, and support replies. No marketing newsletters. You can't opt out of transactional emails while you have an account.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or get a copy — email support from your account address. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have additional rights under those laws; we honor them for everyone and don't discriminate for exercising them. In the past 12 months we have collected identifiers (email, account and Stripe IDs, IP address), and commercial information (subscription and billing history). We have not collected the documents you open in StockStart; they stay on your device. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context advertising. Our sites don't respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals because they don't track visitors.

StockStart is a business product and isn't directed at children under 16.

Where data is kept

Our providers store data in the United States. Using StockStart from outside the US sends your account data to the US. Your documents stay on your device.

Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here and email the address on your account about material changes. The effective date at the top tells you which version you're reading.
